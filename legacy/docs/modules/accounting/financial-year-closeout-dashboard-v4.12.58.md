# Financial Year Closeout Dashboard — v4.12.58

## Page

`/financial-year-closeout`

Menu:

- Accounting → FY Closeout

## What it solves

The app now has many module-level acceptance pages: Purchase Import, Vyapar Sale Import, Accounting/GST Validation, Billing Final QA, Customer Dues Reco, Vendor Payable Reco, Goods Return Acceptance, Payroll Finalization, Day Book export and Print Final Acceptance.

This dashboard combines the period-close evidence into one final screen so the accountant/owner can decide whether the period or financial year is ready to lock.

## Checks included

1. Sales and billing evidence.
2. Purchase and vendor evidence.
3. GST and accounting evidence.
4. Customer dues and credit-note evidence.
5. Vendor payable and debit-note evidence.
6. Goods-return credit expiry evidence.
7. Inventory negative stock evidence.
8. Payroll close-month evidence.
9. Purchase import acceptance evidence.
10. Financial year lock evidence.

## CSV export

Use **CSV evidence** to save the closeout file with the accountant records.

## Limitations

- It does not file GST returns.
- It does not create the FY lock.
- It does not repair missing accounting postings.
- Physical stock verification and bank statement matching remain manual acceptance tasks.
